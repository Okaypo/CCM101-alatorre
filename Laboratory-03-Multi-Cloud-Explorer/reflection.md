The Multi-Cloud Explorer mission helped me understand how AWS, Azure, and GCP can be compared based on their services and business needs. I found GCP interesting because of its AI, machine learning, data analytics, and Kubernetes features.

I learned that these cloud platforms provide many similar services, such as computing, storage, networking, databases, and security. The best platform depends on factors like budget, existing technology, workload, scalability, and company goals.

This activity taught me that choosing a cloud provider should be based on the client's needs rather than popularity. It also helped me improve my GitHub portfolio by adding research, comparisons, recommendations, screenshots, and other evidence of my work.
